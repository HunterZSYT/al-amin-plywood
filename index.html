Al-Amin Plywood Industries (Pvt.) Ltd. is a proudly Bangladeshi manufacturer of plywood sheets and wooden doors, serving furniture makers, developers, contractors, dealers, and institutional clients across the country.

Our Process

From Raw Timber to Reliable Plywood & Wooden Doors

Our production process combines raw material selection, modern machinery, controlled pressing, finishing, and quality inspection to deliver durable plywood sheets and wooden doors for every project need.

01

Raw Material Selection

Carefully selected timber, veneer, and adhesive materials are prepared to ensure strength, bonding quality, and product consistency.

02

Peeling & Veneer Drying

Logs are peeled into veneer sheets and dried through controlled systems to prepare them for stable plywood production.

03

Glue Application & Hot Pressing

Veneer layers are bonded with resin adhesives and pressed under controlled heat and pressure for strength and durability.

04

Finishing & Quality Check

Sheets and doors are cut, sanded, finished, inspected, and packed for reliable delivery to dealers, developers, and bulk buyers.
