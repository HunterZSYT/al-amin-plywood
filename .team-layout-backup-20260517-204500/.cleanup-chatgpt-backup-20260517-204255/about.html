Built on family leadership, industrial discipline, and a commitment to reliable plywood and wooden door manufacturing since 2007.

Al-Amin Plywood Industries began with a vision to serve Bangladesh’s growing construction, furniture, housing, and infrastructure sectors with durable and affordable wood-based products. Today, the company operates with strong production capacity, skilled manpower, dealer support, and a long-term focus on quality, consistency, and customer trust.

Our Mission

To manufacture reliable plywood, veneer boards, and wooden door solutions that support Bangladesh’s housing, furniture, construction, and infrastructure sectors with consistent quality and fair value.

  • Durable products for long-term use
  • Consistent supply for dealers and projects

Our Vision

To become one of Bangladesh’s most trusted plywood and wooden door manufacturers by combining industrial scale, modern production, responsible sourcing, and customer-focused service.

  • Expand quality manufacturing nationwide
  • Set stronger standards for plywood production

Core Values

Al-Amin Plywood Industries is built on trust, discipline, quality control, worker responsibility, customer commitment, and continuous improvement in manufacturing.

  • Quality, honesty, and reliability
  • People-first growth and responsible industry

Our Process

From Raw Timber to Reliable Plywood & Wooden Doors

Every product passes through a disciplined manufacturing flow, from raw material selection and veneer preparation to pressing, finishing, inspection, and delivery support.

01

Raw Material Selection

Timber, veneer, resin, and supporting materials are selected with attention to bonding strength, durability, and product consistency.

02

Peeling & Veneer Drying

Logs are processed into veneer sheets and dried under controlled conditions to prepare stable layers for plywood production.

03

Glue Application & Hot Pressing

Veneer layers are bonded with resin adhesive and pressed under controlled heat and pressure to create strong plywood boards.

04

Finishing & Quality Inspection

Products are cut, sanded, finished, checked, packed, and prepared for dealers, developers, furniture makers, and bulk buyers.
